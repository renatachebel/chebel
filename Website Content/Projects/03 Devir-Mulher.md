# Devir-Mulher

## Page introduction

A poetic video about being and becoming a woman, a partnership with Morena Cardoso and Danzamedicina.

## Project notes

Devir-Mulher is a poetic exploration of feminine identity, transformation, and the multifaceted experience of womanhood. Created in partnership with Morena Cardoso and Danzamedicina, this video piece was part of the Cabiria Film Festival 2020.

The work delves into the intimate and universal aspects of being a woman, capturing moments of vulnerability, strength, connection, and self-discovery. Through a series of carefully composed visual narratives, the piece explores themes of bodily autonomy, sisterhood, sensuality, and the cyclical nature of feminine experience.

The collaboration brings together different artistic perspectives to create a visual poem that celebrates the complexity and beauty of feminine existence. The imagery flows between moments of solitude and communion, nature and intimacy, creating a meditative space for reflection on what it means to embody and express femininity in contemporary society.
