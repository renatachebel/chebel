# The Other Side of Hope Tour — Mola Oddity

## Page introduction

Live visual performance, color remixing and documentary work across Mola Oddity's 2024 China tour.

## Short documentary — The tour from the inside

Alongside the live visual work, I documented the journey and the life around the performances. I directed and edited this short documentary as an intimate record of the band, the people travelling with it and the shifting atmosphere of the tour.

## Visual direction and performance — Color as a live instrument

I joined Mola Oddity's The Other Side of Hope tour across China to extend VJ Suave's animation system into the specific emotional world of each song. For the main concerts, I remixed the colors of the animations song by song, reshaping their palettes while preserving the hand-drawn visual language.

During the club circuit I performed the visuals live. I layered multiple effects, built feedback loops and routed the animation material through a virtual modular video synthesizer. The image was never a fixed playback: it was mixed in real time with the same responsiveness as an instrument.

## Concert and festival circuit — From Shanghai to Chengdu

The main tour opened on 29 March 2024 at Bandai Namco Shanghai Dream Hall and continued through Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Nanjing, Hangzhou, Beijing and Chengdu. Festival appearances included Strawberry Music Festival in Fuzhou and Hangzhou, and Chun You Music Festival in Chengdu.

## Source animation — A visual world for eighteen songs

The tour's hand-drawn characters, animals, rainbows and changing environments formed a modular image archive for the performances. The animations were created by Paulo Stoker and Ygor Marotta, with assistance from Lucas Fiacadori. I developed the color treatments used across the songs and transformed this material further in the live mix.

## Club circuit — LOFi MOSH: 9 Days of Dreamcore Aquarium

Running parallel to the concerts and festivals was a second route through China's electronic club scene: ALL Club in Shanghai, LOOPY in Hangzhou, OIL in Shenzhen, Pillbox in Beijing and AXIS in Chengdu.

The more electronic sets called for another visual language. Instead of following a predetermined concert sequence, I improvised with VJ Suave's animation archive, live effects, feedback and modular video synthesis. Each night produced a different, unstable composition.

## Photographic chapter — China Oddities

China Oddities is the photographic diary inside the larger tour project. Shot on film while travelling and working with the band, the series moves away from the scale of the stage and toward backstage preparation, birthdays, quiet moments between cities and the personal textures of touring life.
