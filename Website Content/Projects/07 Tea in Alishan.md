# Tea in Alishan

## Page introduction

A photographic series documenting tea harvesters at work in the misty mountains of Alishan, Taiwan.

## Project notes

Tea in Alishan is a photographic documentation of the traditional tea cultivation in Taiwan's famous Alishan mountain region. The series captures both the breathtaking landscape of the mountain tea gardens and the meticulous labor of the tea harvesters as they work among the shrubs. 

Alishan is renowned for producing some of Taiwan's finest high mountain oolong teas, grown at elevations above 1,000 meters where cloud cover and cooler temperatures create ideal growing conditions. The photographs reveal the harmonious relationship between the tea farmers and their environment, highlighting the manual harvesting process that has been preserved for generations.

The series contrasts the ethereal beauty of the misty mountain landscapes with intimate portraits of the harvesters, their faces often shaded by traditional wide-brimmed hats as they carefully pick only the most tender leaves. These images tell a story of cultural heritage, sustainable agriculture, and the human connection to the land that produces one of Taiwan's most celebrated exports.
