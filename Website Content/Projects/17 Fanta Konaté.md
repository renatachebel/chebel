# Fanta Konaté

## Page introduction

Visual projections created for Fanta Konaté's performance at Festival Ilumina 2023.

## Project notes

Visual projections created for Fanta Konaté, an artist from Guinea, during her powerful performance at Festival Ilumina 2023. The immersive visuals featured vibrant African-inspired imagery, traditional masks, and dynamic patterns that complemented the musical journey, creating a bridge between ancestral traditions and contemporary artistic expression.
