# Fluvial

## Page introduction

An immersive live audiovisual performance that invites audiences to inhabit a submerged Amazonian ecosystem.

## Project notes

Fluvial is an immersive live audiovisual performance developed at the Amazonia Imersiva Art Residency in Belém, Brazil, by Renata Chebel and Glasgow-based musician and composer Sonia Killmann. Inside a 360° projection environment spanning the walls and floor, audiences move freely around a boat-like central platform and enter a submerged Amazonian ecosystem populated by piranhas, manatees, jaguars, and Yaras - Amazonian river spirits.

Grounded in Donna Haraway's concept of "making kin" and Indigenous Amazonian philosophy, the work positions the human as one temporary participant in a dense network of coexistence, vulnerability, and transformation.

AI-generated imagery, animation, expanded cinema, voice, saxophone, modular synthesis, live looping, and local instruments are manipulated in real time. Each 30-40 minute performance becomes a singular, unstable ecosystem, much like the rivers that inspired it.
